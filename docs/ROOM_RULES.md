# Room Rules

- One room is one persistent container.
- Modes: Voice, Video and Game.
- Switching modes does not replace room identity.
- Room ID, owner, admins, audience, chat, gifts, VIP state, rankings and event state persist.
- Level 1 starts with 8 seats.
- Higher levels unlock 12, 16, 20 and up to 27 seats.
- Owner/admin/moderator capability is available from Level 1.
